Itinerary outline

Food-first pacing. Exact restaurants and market days flex with season, weather, and group energy—never with shopping commissions.

Day 1

Arrive Chengdu. Soft landing: neighborhood walk, tea or light supper, trip briefing. No marathon sightseeing on arrival night.

Day 2

Chengdu food foundations—chili, peppercorn, and regional styles—plus a market or street-food circuit with context, not a checklist sprint.

Day 3

Hands-on cooking session and/or deeper market tasting. Learn a few dishes you can recreate at home.

Day 4

Tea culture and slower Chengdu: teahouse time, parks or local neighborhoods, and a thoughtfully paced dinner.

Day 5

Optional panda morning (booking dependent) or a free half-day. Afternoon continue food & local-life program in Chengdu.

Day 6

Travel to Chongqing. First taste of river-city flavors—hotpot context, street snacks, skyline and neighborhood feel.

Day 7

Chongqing deep dive: markets, noodles, and local spots chosen for quality—not commission. Time to walk and eat at human pace.

Day 8–9

Buffer / flex days: more Chongqing or Chengdu wrap-up depending on 8- vs 9-day version, departures, and group preference. Final dinner and clear send-off notes.

This is an outline, not a locked hourly schedule. You receive a written day-by-day with inclusions before any deposit.

What’s included vs not

Transparent all-in vs not-included. No vague “many meals” language without a count.

Typically included

  • Small-group guiding & food program as described in your written terms
  • Cooking session / market experiences listed for your departure
  • Intercity transfer Chengdu ↔ Chongqing as specified
  • Listed meals (exact count in your quote)
  • Entrance fees for activities listed as included
  • Planning support in English before arrival

Typically not included

  • International flights to/from China
  • China visa fees
  • Travel insurance (strongly recommended)
  • Hotels—unless your written quote says otherwise
  • Personal snacks, drinks, and optional purchases
  • Optional panda tickets if not listed as included
  • Single-room supplements or private add-ons
Your quote wins. The written inclusions list for your dates is the contract—not this web outline. If something matters to you (hotel level, dietary needs, panda morning), ask before you pay.

Group size & model

6 to confirm, 10 max

We don’t run oversized buses. Small group means better tables and clearer guiding.
