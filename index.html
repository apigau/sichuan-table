FAQ

Straight answers—no fake reviews, no urgency timers.

Who am I actually booking with?

You book with Sichuan Table. A licensed Sichuan–Chongqing agency runs the ground logistics. Partner license details are published before you pay so you can verify them yourself.

Will there be forced shopping stops?

No. We do not include commission shopping shops. Optional purchases (snacks, tea, knives, etc.) are always your choice.

When do I pay, and how?

Deposit only after you receive written terms. Familiar payments (Stripe / PayPal deposit) are planned. We never ask for crypto or opaque personal bank wires without a contract.

How big is the group?

We confirm the departure at 6 guests and cap at 10. Small enough to eat well and move without a megaphone.

Is this medical tourism?

No. The culinary trip is separate from our optional hospital accompaniment / translation assist for expats already in Chengdu. See the medical escort note—it is not medical advice.
