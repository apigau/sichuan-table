Why trust Sichuan Table

Many visitors worry about scams in China tourism: hidden commissions, forced shopping, and unclear payments. Here is exactly how we work—and what we will never ask you to do.

1

Real person, Chengdu-based

Sichuan Table is operated from Chengdu. You talk to a real person about dates, diet, and pace—not an anonymous overseas call center.

2

Licensed ground partner

You book with Sichuan Table; a licensed Sichuan–Chongqing agency runs the ground. Partner license details published before you pay so you can verify them.

3

No forced shopping shops

We do not pad the day with commission showrooms. Markets and makers are for learning and tasting. Buying is optional—always.

4

Deposit only after written terms

Price, inclusions, exclusions, and cancel/refund rules come in writing first. No deposit before you have that document.

Partner license

We will not invent a license number on this site.

Partner license details published before you pay. License number and agency name fields are intentionally left blank here until ready (TBD). Before any deposit, you receive the partner’s details so you can check them yourself.

Blank on purpose—honesty beats a fabricated number.

Payments (planned)

Familiar rails. Clear amounts.

Stripe / PayPal deposit

We plan to take deposits via familiar platforms (Stripe and/or PayPal). Status: planned—confirmed in your written terms for your departure.

You know the price before you pay

All-in trip price (as quoted) vs itemized not-included list. No “pay cash to the driver for mystery extras” as a business model.

Cancel & refund rules (draft)

Draft policy for transparency. Your written booking terms for a specific departure supersede this page.

  • Before deposit: Ask anything; no payment owed.
  • After deposit, trip not yet confirmed (under 6 guests): Deposit handling stated in your written terms—typically refundable minus any non-recoverable third-party costs already agreed in writing.
  • After trip confirmed (6+ guests): Tiered refund windows by days before start date—exact percentages in your contract.
  • Operator cancel: If we cancel your departure, you receive a refund of amounts paid for the cancelled services per written terms (or a mutually agreed move to another date).
  • Force majeure: Treated per written terms; we communicate early and in English.

This is a draft outline for trust—not legal advice and not your final contract. Final cancel/refund schedule is in the document you approve before paying.

What we never ask

Hard lines. If anyone claiming to be us asks for these, stop and contact us on the email published on this site.

Never

  • Cryptocurrency payments
  • Opaque personal bank wires without a written contract
  • Deposit before you have written terms
  • Fake “must buy today” urgency or fabricated reviews
  • Forced shopping shop visits as part of the tour

Instead

  • Written quote + inclusions/exclusions
  • Partner license details before payment
  • Familiar deposit methods (Stripe/PayPal—planned)
  • Clear cancel/refund schedule in writing
  • Chengdu-based human contact
